# Bolzano Street Food Tour — Photo Library Reference Guide

**Purpose:** A curated, theme-organized starting point for sourcing photography for the new site. I've previewed representative images inline in our conversation above; this document gives you direct, one-click search links into **free-to-use, commercial-license stock libraries** so your team (or I, in a follow-up step) can pull final hi-res files.

## Why links instead of bundled files
I don't have a way to download and repackage arbitrary web images into files for you — my working environment has no general internet/download access, and most images surfaced by a general web image search are professional/news/travel-blog photography that is **copyrighted to someone else**, not free for reuse on a commercial tourism site. Reusing those without a license is a real legal risk for the business.

The clean, safe way to build this library is from libraries that grant free commercial-use licenses: **Unsplash, Pexels, and Pixabay**. Below are direct search links per theme — click through, and you (or I, if you paste me the URLs of the ones you pick) can download the originals at full resolution.

## Recommended next step
Once you (or your local guides/team) have actual on-location photography — which is strongly preferable for an authentic tour-operator brand rather than generic stock — that should replace stock images wherever possible, especially for guide portraits, specific tasting stops, and the actual meeting points. Stock photography is best reserved for broad "mood" shots (mountain vistas, wine-country landscapes) rather than anything claiming to show *this specific tour*.
